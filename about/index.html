About Sauna & Plunge

SaunaAndPlunge.com is an independent website covering cold plunges, home saunas, and gym sauna access. We write honest, practical guides designed to help you make smarter decisions — whether you're building your first cold plunge at home, shopping for a sauna, or just trying to figure out which gym in your city has a decent steam room.

What We Cover

The site is organized around three areas:

  • Cold Plunge — DIY setups, buying guides, protocols, and equipment reviews
  • Home Sauna — cost guides, setup advice, and product recommendations
  • Gym Guide — which major gym chains have saunas and steam rooms, and how to find one near you

Our Approach

We don't accept payment for placement or reviews. Guides are based on publicly available research, manufacturer specifications, and collective practitioner experience in the cold water immersion and sauna communities.

Some links on this site are affiliate links — if you buy something through one, we may earn a small commission at no additional cost to you. This is how the site sustains itself. It doesn't influence what we recommend.

Health Disclaimer

Information on this site is for educational purposes only. Cold water immersion and sauna use carry real physiological effects and are not appropriate for everyone. Consult a physician before starting any new health protocol, particularly if you have cardiovascular conditions, hypertension, or other health concerns.
