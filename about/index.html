About The Sauna & Plunge

Quick Answer

The Sauna & Plunge is an independent resource for anyone exploring cold plunge therapy, home saunas, and finding gym sauna access. We focus on accurate, research-grounded information without paid placement or sponsored rankings.

What This Site Is

The Sauna & Plunge exists to answer the questions people actually have about heat and cold therapy — without the hype, the unsupported claims, or the thinly veiled product pitches that dominate most wellness content.

We cover three main areas: cold plunge therapy, home saunas, and finding gym saunas. Within those areas, we try to give you the most accurate, useful information available based on published research and real-world experience.

Our Approach

When we say something produces a specific physiological effect, we try to trace that back to actual research — not to a podcast episode or a supplement brand's website. We cite studies from peer-reviewed journals and flag where the evidence is strong versus where it's preliminary or contested.

We also try to be honest about what we don't know. Cold therapy research is a rapidly evolving field, and findings from 2018 are sometimes contradicted or refined by 2023 studies. We aim to keep our content current and to acknowledge uncertainty where it exists.

Our editorial recommendations are not influenced by affiliate relationships; we will tell you when a product isn't worth the money even if we have an affiliate link for it.

Contact

Have a question, correction, or suggestion? Reach out through the site. We genuinely read feedback and it often shapes future content. If you find a factual error on any page, please let us know — accuracy matters more than ego.
