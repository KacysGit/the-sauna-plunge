How to Get Your Site Live

This folder contains your complete, ready-to-deploy website for the-sauna-plunge.com (or whatever domain you choose). Here's exactly what to do to get it live. Total time: about 45 minutes.

What You Need to Do (Overview)

  1. Register a domain name (~$10–$12, one-time)
  2. Create a free Netlify account (free hosting — no monthly cost)
  3. Upload this folder to Netlify (drag and drop)
  4. Connect your domain to Netlify (free)
  5. Set up Google Search Console (free)
  6. Apply for Google AdSense (free, when ready)

Step 1: Register Your Domain

1

Go to namecheap.com. Search for the-sauna-plunge.com. If available, register it for ~$10–$12/year. If taken, try thecoldplungeguide.com or plungeinsider.com.

When asked about hosting, you don't need it from Namecheap — you'll use Netlify for free hosting. Just buy the domain.

Create an account and keep your login credentials safe.

Step 2: Create a Free Netlify Account

2

Go to netlify.com and click Sign up. Use your email or sign up with GitHub. The free plan is all you need — it includes:

  • Free static site hosting
  • Free SSL certificate (https://)
  • Custom domain support
  • 100GB bandwidth/month

Step 3: Deploy the Site

3

In the Netlify dashboard, you'll see a large area that says "Drag and drop your site folder here". Open your file manager or Finder, find the the-sauna-plunge-site folder, and drag the entire folder onto that area in Netlify.

Netlify will upload all the files and give you a temporary URL like https://quirky-fox-abc123.netlify.app. Open that URL and make sure your site looks correct.

Step 4: Connect Your Custom Domain

4

In the Netlify dashboard for your site, go to Domain settings → Add a custom domain. Enter the-sauna-plunge.com.

Netlify will give you nameserver addresses (like dns1.p01.nsone.net). Go back to Namecheap, find your domain, and update the nameservers to Netlify's. Instructions vary slightly but the Namecheap control panel is straightforward.

DNS changes take 15 minutes to 48 hours to propagate. Your site will be live at your custom domain once this completes. Netlify automatically handles the free SSL certificate.

Step 5: Set Up Google Search Console

5

Go to search.google.com/search-console and add your site as a new property. Verify ownership using the HTML file method (Google gives you a small file to upload — just drop it in your site folder and redeploy to Netlify).

Once verified, submit a sitemap. Your sitemap should be at: the-sauna-plunge.com/sitemap.xml (create this file — see below).

Step 7: Add Google AdSense (When Ready)

7

Wait until you have at least 10–15 published articles before applying. Go to adsense.google.com and apply. Google will review your site (usually 1–2 weeks) and either approve or request changes.

Once approved, Google gives you a small JavaScript snippet to add to your site. Add it to the <head> section of each page's HTML (or globally via Netlify's asset injection). This enables auto-ads — Google will automatically place ads in relevant locations.

The ad slots already exist in the HTML — they're labeled "Advertisement" as placeholders. When AdSense is active, Google's script will fill those spaces with real ads.

Step 8: Add New Articles Over Time

8

To add a new article:

  1. Create a new folder in the site directory (e.g., does-equinox-have-sauna)
  2. Create an index.html file inside that folder, following the same structure as the existing articles
  3. Add the link to the sitemap.xml file
  4. Redeploy to Netlify (drag the updated folder again)
