Does 24 Hour Fitness Have a Sauna?

Quick Answer

It depends on the club tier. 24 Hour Fitness Sport and Super Sport locations typically have dry saunas and/or steam rooms. Standard Active locations may not. Check the specific club's amenity list on the 24 Hour Fitness website before joining.

Does 24 Hour Fitness Have a Sauna?

24 Hour Fitness is one of the largest gym chains in the US. Their higher-tier Sport and Super Sport clubs have historically been known for comprehensive amenities including saunas, pools, and racquetball courts. If sauna access is important to you, filter specifically for Sport or Super Sport locations.

What Type of Sauna Does 24 Hour Fitness Have?

24 Hour Fitness operates several club tiers: Active, Sport, and Super Sport. Sauna and steam room availability scales with tier — Super Sport locations most reliably have them, while basic Active locations often don't. Membership prices and amenities vary accordingly.

24 Hour Fitness Membership Cost and Sauna Access

Standard Active memberships start around $25–35/month. Sport tier clubs run $35–50/month. Super Sport, with the best amenities including sauna and pool, is typically $50–80/month. Verify your local club's tier and amenity list on the 24 Hour Fitness website.

Alternatives If Your 24 Hour Fitness Doesn't Have a Sauna

Not every location is created equal — amenities vary by individual club, age of facility, and local franchise decisions. If your nearest 24 Hour Fitness doesn't have a sauna, the best alternatives are:

  • LA Fitness: Most locations have a dry sauna and/or steam room. Widely considered the most reliable sauna gym chain in the US.
  • YMCA: Many branches have saunas — call your local Y to confirm. Day passes often available.
  • 24 Hour Fitness (Sport/Super Sport): Upper-tier memberships include sauna access at most facilities.
  • Life Time Fitness / Equinox: Premium chains that consistently offer sauna, steam, and sometimes cold plunge. Higher membership cost but excellent facilities.
  • Day spa or Korean spa: $20–50 day pass for unlimited sauna access without a membership.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does every 24 Hour Fitness location have a sauna?
No — sauna availability varies by location. Always call or check the specific club's amenity list on the 24 Hour Fitness website before joining specifically for sauna access.
How do I find a gym with a sauna near me?
Search "gym with sauna near me" on Google Maps, then filter for higher-tier chains (LA Fitness, YMCA, 24 Hour Fitness, Life Time). Check individual location pages for amenities, and always confirm by phone before visiting.
Does 24 Hour Fitness have a steam room?
Many Sport and Super Sport locations have steam rooms in addition to dry saunas. As with sauna availability, this depends on the specific club tier and location. Check the club page on the 24 Hour Fitness website for confirmed amenities.
