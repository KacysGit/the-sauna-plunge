Does LA Fitness Have a Sauna?

Updated June 2025 Gym Guide 5 min read

Quick Answer

Yes — most LA Fitness locations have a dry sauna, and many also have a steam room and whirlpool. LA Fitness is widely considered one of the best mainstream gym chains for sauna access at an affordable price (~$30–$35/month). If you're looking for a gym with reliable sauna access without paying Life Time or Equinox prices, LA Fitness is the most frequently recommended option.

Unlike Planet Fitness (no sauna anywhere) or Anytime Fitness (franchise-dependent), LA Fitness has a consistent, corporate-managed standard that includes wet area amenities at the vast majority of its roughly 700 locations.

What Sauna Facilities Does LA Fitness Have?

Most LA Fitness locations offer the following in their locker rooms:

  • Dry sauna — available in both men's and women's locker rooms at most locations. Typically a cedar-lined room with electric heater, running 160–185°F. No reservation needed — first-come, first-served.
  • Steam room — also in the locker rooms at many locations. A wet steam environment, typically 110–120°F with high humidity. Easier for some people to tolerate than a dry sauna.
  • Whirlpool / hot tub — many LA Fitness locations have a pool area that includes a whirlpool, separate from the locker room sauna.

Included at No Extra Cost

These amenities are included in the standard LA Fitness membership — there's no upgrade required. They're part of what you get at the base price.

What to Expect at LA Fitness: Practical Tips

  • Peak hours matter. Saunas are shared spaces. Evenings (5–8 PM) are the busiest. If you have flexibility, a mid-morning or early afternoon session will give you more breathing room.
  • Bring a towel. Always sit on a towel in a gym sauna — both for hygiene and courtesy. Many gym-goers bring a second towel for wiping down.
  • Hydrate beforehand. Drink 1–2 glasses of water before a sauna session. You'll sweat significantly in a dry sauna at 160–185°F.
  • Don't use your phone in the sauna. High heat can permanently damage phone batteries and displays. Leave it in your locker.
  • Sauna + cold contrast at home. LA Fitness doesn't have cold plunges, but you can recreate contrast therapy at home after your sauna by using a cold shower or a DIY cold plunge.

Does LA Fitness Have a Cold Plunge?

Currently, LA Fitness does not have cold plunges at standard locations. Cold plunges are an emerging amenity in the gym industry — Life Time Fitness and some high-end wellness clubs are beginning to add them, but mainstream chains like LA Fitness haven't widely adopted them yet.

If you want a sauna + cold plunge combination, your options are: a premium gym like Life Time (~$100–$200/month), a dedicated wellness spa, or setting up a cold plunge at home to complement your LA Fitness sauna sessions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does LA Fitness have a sauna?
Yes. Most LA Fitness locations have a dry sauna included with the standard membership. Many also have a steam room. Amenities are corporate-standardized, so availability is much more consistent than franchise chains.
Is LA Fitness sauna included in the base membership?
Yes. Sauna access is included in the standard LA Fitness membership at no extra charge. There's no upgrade tier required.
What temperature is the LA Fitness sauna?
LA Fitness dry saunas typically run between 160–185°F (71–85°C). The exact temperature varies by location and time of day.
How long should I stay in the LA Fitness sauna?
For most healthy adults, 15–20 minutes per session is a good starting point. Listen to your body — if you feel dizzy, light-headed, or overly uncomfortable, exit immediately. Stay hydrated.
Does LA Fitness have a cold plunge?
No. Cold plunges are not currently a standard LA Fitness amenity. Consider a home DIY cold plunge to complement your gym sauna sessions.
Does every LA Fitness have a sauna?
Most do, but it's not 100% guaranteed at every location. Older or smaller club footprints may not have the full wet area. Always check the specific location's amenity list on the LA Fitness website or call ahead before joining specifically for the sauna.
