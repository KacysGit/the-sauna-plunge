Mediavine Upgrade Path

Google AdSense pays ~$3–8 RPM. Mediavine pays ~$20–35 RPM but requires 50,000 monthly sessions. Once you hit that milestone (realistically months 8–14 with consistent content), switching to Mediavine will 3–5x your ad revenue overnight without changing a single line of content. Track sessions in Google Analytics from day one so you know when you cross the threshold.

Quick Wins This Month

While working through the content calendar, do these one-time setup tasks that compound over time:

  • Submit sitemap.xml to Google Search Console (instructions in DEPLOY-INSTRUCTIONS.html)
  • Apply to Google AdSense (requires a few published articles and a privacy policy — add one to the About page)
  • Apply to Amazon Associates — takes 1 week; you have 180 days to make 3 qualifying sales
  • Set up a free Google Analytics 4 property and add the tracking snippet to site.js
  • Submit the site URL to Bing Webmaster Tools (free; often forgotten; adds 10–15% more traffic)
  • Contact SaunaLife and Dundalk directly for affiliate approval — they approve quickly for new sites
