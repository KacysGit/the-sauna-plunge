Cluster 1: Gym Sauna Guides (Top Traffic Driver)

These "does [gym] have a sauna" pages are the site's #1 traffic cluster. The LA Fitness page already gets 846 impressions at position ~20. The pattern is proven — every major gym chain is a new page opportunity. Low difficulty because they're informational with a specific answer, and established SEO sites ignore them.
⚠️ Key competitor: gymswithsauna.com — ranks for nearly every gym chain query. Beat them with longer pages: specific location counts, membership prices, steam room vs. dry sauna breakdown, and which specific locations have them.
