Gyms With Saunas Near Me: Best Chains & How to Find One

Quick Answer

The best gym chains for sauna access are LA Fitness (most reliable), YMCA (many locations), 24 Hour Fitness Sport/Super Sport, and premium options like Life Time Fitness and Equinox. Planet Fitness does not have saunas. Always verify your specific location before joining.

Best Gym Chains With Saunas: Ranked by Availability and Value

Not all gym chains deliver equally on sauna access. Here's an honest ranking based on consistency, quality, and cost:

  • Life Time Fitness / Equinox (Tier 1 — Premium): The gold standard for gym saunas in the US. Almost every Life Time and Equinox location has a full dry sauna, steam room, and increasingly, a cold plunge pool. Facilities are well-maintained, spacious, and run at proper temperatures (175–185°F). Expect to pay $100–200+/month, but the sauna experience genuinely rivals dedicated spas.
  • LA Fitness (Tier 2 — Best Value): The most consistent sauna-equipped major gym chain in the US. Most locations have a dry sauna and steam room included in a $35–45/month membership. The sauna quality varies by location age and maintenance, but the availability is unmatched at this price point. See our LA Fitness sauna guide.
  • YMCA (Tier 2 — Community Value): Many YMCA branches have saunas, especially larger full-service locations with aquatics centers. Nonprofit pricing with income-based assistance makes this accessible. Quality ranges from excellent (newer renovated branches) to dated (older neighborhood Ys). Always call ahead. See our YMCA sauna guide.
  • 24 Hour Fitness Sport/Super Sport (Tier 2 — Good for 24/7): Reliable sauna access at Sport and Super Sport tier locations. Good for early morning or late night sauna use. Verify your specific club's tier before joining. See our 24 Hour Fitness sauna guide.
  • Gold's Gym (Tier 3 — Variable): Some locations have saunas, but it's not consistent across the chain. Franchise model means amenity decisions vary by owner. Worth checking your specific location.
  • Anytime Fitness (Tier 3 — Variable): Some franchise owners have invested in sauna facilities; many haven't. The 24/7 access is a real advantage where saunas exist. See our Anytime Fitness sauna guide.
  • Planet Fitness (Not Recommended for Sauna): No saunas. Not available at any membership tier. If sauna access is your goal, Planet Fitness is the wrong choice. See our Planet Fitness sauna guide for alternatives.

How to Find a Gym With a Sauna Near You — Step by Step

The most reliable method for finding a sauna-equipped gym in your area:

  • Step 1: Search Google Maps for "gym with sauna near me." Look at the results and note which chains appear.
  • Step 2: For each chain that appears, navigate to the chain's official website and find the specific location's amenity listing (not just the chain's general page). LA Fitness, YMCA, and 24 Hour Fitness all have location-specific amenity pages.
  • Step 3: Call ahead to confirm the sauna is currently operational. Saunas in gyms go out of service for maintenance, repairs, or seasonal closures — and the website listing may not reflect the current status. One phone call saves you a wasted trip.
  • Step 4: Use a free trial or day pass to evaluate the sauna quality before committing to a membership. Temperature, cleanliness, and crowding all matter.

For non-gym sauna options in your area, search "Korean spa near me," "banya near me," or "sauna studio near me." These often provide a superior sauna experience at $20–60 per day pass with no membership commitment.

What Makes a Good Gym Sauna

When you visit a potential gym on a trial basis, evaluate the sauna on these criteria:

  • Temperature: A proper dry sauna runs 160–185°F (71–85°C). Ask staff or bring a small thermometer. Anything consistently below 155°F is underperforming. If the heater can't reach 160°F, the therapeutic benefit is significantly reduced.
  • Materials: Cedar-lined interiors are ideal — the wood absorbs moisture and resists bacteria better than synthetic alternatives. Avoid saunas with plastic paneling or fiberglass benches.
  • Cleanliness and ventilation: A well-maintained sauna shouldn't smell like mildew or accumulated sweat. Good ventilation cycles air without making the room drafty. Stained benches or a persistent musty smell indicate poor maintenance.
  • Capacity and crowding: A 4-person sauna at a 2,000-member gym will be crowded at peak times. Evaluate whether the sauna is large enough for the volume of members or if you'll be waiting regularly.
  • Rocks and löyly capability: If you want to pour water over the rocks (löyly, the Finnish steam technique), verify the heater style allows it. Some electric heaters are not designed for water and will be damaged — the gym may prohibit this. Purpose-built kiuas heaters support löyly.

Day Passes vs. Gym Memberships for Sauna Access

For occasional sauna use (1–2 times per month), day passes ($10–30) and per-session Korean spa visits ($25–60) are more cost-effective than a full gym membership. You get access to a high-quality sauna without the monthly commitment.

For regular use (3+ times per week), a gym membership almost always beats per-session pricing. LA Fitness at ~$40/month works out to under $4 per sauna session if you go 10+ times monthly — significantly cheaper than any spa. The math tilts toward membership quickly if sauna use is a consistent part of your routine.

If you already have a gym membership at a chain without a sauna (Planet Fitness, basic Anytime Fitness), consider whether it's worth supplementing with a Korean spa membership or occasional day pass visits rather than switching gyms entirely.

For quick verification of sauna availability at specific locations, use each chain's official finder: LA Fitness locations, YMCA branches, and 24 Hour Fitness clubs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which gym has the best sauna?
Life Time Fitness and Equinox consistently have the highest-quality sauna facilities in the US gym market — well-maintained, properly heated, spacious. For the best value at a reasonable price, LA Fitness is the most reliable option. Korean spas and jjimjilbangs often surpass gym saunas on ambiance and experience for a day-pass price.
Can I use the gym sauna without working out?
Yes — most gyms allow members to use the sauna without using the gym floor. You're a member; the sauna is part of the facilities. Some locations have specific sauna hours or separate access procedures. Check your gym's posted rules or ask at the front desk.
What's the average temperature of a gym sauna?
Most US gym saunas run between 160–180°F (71–82°C). The best ones hit 175–185°F consistently. Below 155°F, the therapeutic benefit (heat stress response, sweating, cardiovascular benefit) is significantly diminished. If your gym's sauna consistently underperforms on temperature, it's worth flagging to management or reconsidering the facility.
How do I find a 24-hour gym with a sauna near me?
24 Hour Fitness Sport and Super Sport locations offer 24-hour access with sauna facilities. Anytime Fitness locations with saunas are also 24/7. Use each chain's club finder to identify Sport-tier 24 Hour Fitness locations near you, then confirm sauna hours by phone — some restrict sauna access during overnight hours for safety.
