Costco Saunas: Are They Worth It in 2025?

Quick Answer

Costco periodically sells barrel saunas and infrared cabin saunas, typically for $1,500–4,500. The value is solid for occasional buyers who want a recognizable brand (Dynamic, Radiant) at a discounted price. However, Costco's sauna selection is limited, seasonal, and unavailable year-round. Specialty retailers offer more models, better support, and comparable pricing.

What Saunas Does Costco Sell?

Costco's sauna inventory is seasonal and limited — they typically carry 3–6 models at any given time, primarily from brands like Dynamic Sauna, Radiant Sauna, and Dundalk LeisureCraft. Common offerings include:

  • Infrared sauna cabins: 2–4 person units, usually 4–8 low/mid/full-spectrum infrared panels. Priced at $1,800–3,500. These represent the best of Costco's sauna value.
  • Barrel saunas: Western red cedar barrel saunas for 2–4 people, typically traditional/wood-burning or electric. Priced at $2,500–4,500.
  • Outdoor cabin saunas: Larger capacity 4–6 person cedar cabin saunas. Priced at $3,500–6,000 when available.

Availability varies widely by season and warehouse location. Costco also sells online (Costco.com) with a wider selection than in-store.

Costco Sauna Pros and Cons

Pros: Recognized brand names, Costco's industry-leading return policy (often 90 days or more), competitive pricing on models they carry, and the convenience of one-stop shopping if you're already a Costco member.

Cons: Limited selection, seasonal availability (many models sell out), no showroom to try before you buy, and customer service routes through both Costco and the manufacturer. Specialty sauna retailers often offer similar pricing with much deeper product knowledge and support.

How Costco Prices Compare

On the models Costco does carry, pricing is typically 10–20% below the manufacturer's suggested retail price (MSRP) — consistent with Costco's value model. However, specialist sauna retailers like Almost Heaven, Dundalk, and SaunaSpace frequently run sales that match or beat Costco's pricing, and offer a far wider selection.

Should You Buy a Sauna from Costco?

Buy from Costco if: you're already a member, you spot a model that fits your needs at a competitive price, and you value Costco's return policy as a safety net for a significant purchase.

Look elsewhere if: you want a specific model not currently in Costco's rotation, you want expert guidance on what type of sauna is right for your space, or you need a unit quickly (Costco's seasonal inventory may not have what you want).

Costco Sauna Brands: Are They Any Good?

The brands Costco typically carries for saunas are legitimate mid-market manufacturers:

  • Dynamic Sauna: One of the most popular mid-range infrared sauna brands in the US. Their cabins use low-EMF carbon far-infrared panels and are known for even heat distribution. Build quality is solid for the price — cedar wood construction, digital controls, chromotherapy lighting. A good pick for casual to moderate infrared sauna users.
  • Radiant Sauna: Canadian hemlock and red cedar constructions with medium-quality infrared panel setups. Generally well-reviewed for assembly experience and value. Customer service is mixed depending on the specific retailer (Costco's support channel vs. Radiant's direct support).
  • Dundalk LeisureCraft: Canadian-made outdoor barrel and cabin saunas with solid construction. The barrel saunas in particular are well-regarded in the enthusiast community for their authentic traditional sauna experience and cedar quality.

None of these brands are premium — they're mid-market. For the price point, build quality and heat performance are generally appropriate. Don't expect Harvia heater quality or Helo craftsmanship at Costco sauna prices. Do expect a functional, attractive sauna that will serve most users well for 10+ years with basic maintenance.

Costco Sauna vs. Specialty Retailer

The tradeoffs between buying at Costco versus a specialty sauna retailer like Almost Heaven, SaunaSpace, or a dedicated infrared sauna shop:

  • Price: Costco wins or ties on models they carry (10–20% below MSRP typically). Specialty retailers run comparable sales but are more expensive at full price.
  • Selection: Specialty retailers win decisively. Costco carries 3–8 models at any time; specialty retailers carry dozens of configurations.
  • Expertise: Specialty retailers win significantly. Sauna-specific staff can help you match a unit to your space, budget, and usage goals. Costco warehouse staff cannot.
  • Return policy: Costco wins for the safety net (90-day return on most items). Specialty retailer return policies vary widely.
  • Long-term support: Specialty retailers often have deeper after-sale support, warranty handling, and parts availability. Worth factoring in for a major purchase.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Costco have good saunas?
The brands Costco carries — Dynamic, Radiant, Dundalk — are legitimate, mid-market sauna manufacturers with decent quality. They're not premium, but they're not cheap knockoffs either. For casual to moderate sauna users, they're a reasonable choice.
Can I return a sauna to Costco?
Costco's standard return policy allows returns on most items, and for large appliances like saunas, they typically offer a 90-day return window. Contact Costco directly to confirm the specific return terms for the sauna model you're considering.
What brands does Costco sell for saunas?
The most common sauna brands at Costco are Dynamic Sauna, Radiant Sauna, and Dundalk LeisureCraft. Availability changes seasonally, so check Costco.com for the current inventory.
