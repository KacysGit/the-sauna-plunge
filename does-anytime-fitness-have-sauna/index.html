Does Anytime Fitness Have a Sauna?

Updated June 2025Gym Guide5 min read
Quick Answer

It depends on the location. Anytime Fitness is a franchise, meaning each owner operates independently and chooses their own amenities. Some locations have saunas — most don't. Your best move is to call your local club before signing up.

With over 5,000 locations across the United States, Anytime Fitness is one of the most accessible gym chains in the country. But its franchise model creates a frustrating inconsistency when it comes to amenities: what's available at one location may not exist at the next one.

Saunas fall squarely into this "depends on the owner" category. Unlike corporate chains such as LA Fitness or Life Time — which build to a standard floor plan — Anytime Fitness franchisees make their own decisions about which upgrades to invest in.

How to Find Out if Your Anytime Fitness Has a Sauna

Here are the three most reliable ways to check before you commit to a membership:

  1. Call the club directly. This is the fastest and most reliable method. Ask specifically: "Does your location have a sauna available for members?" A simple yes/no answer takes 30 seconds.
  2. Use the Anytime Fitness Club Finder. Go to anytimefitness.com, find your local club, and look at the listed amenities. Some clubs list their sauna on their location page — but not all clubs keep this up to date.
  3. Take a free tour. Most Anytime Fitness locations offer a free tour before you sign up. Walk through and see the facilities yourself. This also gives you a chance to assess the overall quality of the equipment and cleanliness.

Don't Trust the Website Alone

Franchise location pages on the Anytime Fitness website are managed by the individual owner and are frequently out of date. Amenities that were added or removed months ago may not be reflected online. Always confirm by phone or in person.

What Amenities Does Anytime Fitness Typically Offer?

While sauna availability varies, most Anytime Fitness locations offer a consistent core set of amenities:

  • 24/7 access — the defining feature of the brand; you can work out at any hour with your key fob
  • Cardio equipment — treadmills, ellipticals, stationary bikes, rowing machines
  • Strength equipment — free weights, cable machines, resistance machines
  • Tanning beds — available at many (but not all) locations; usually costs extra
  • Locker rooms with showers — standard at all locations
  • Personal training — available to purchase separately
  • Reciprocal use — most Anytime Fitness memberships let you use other locations, including international clubs

Saunas, steam rooms, and pools are "premium" upgrades that franchise owners can add if they choose to invest in them. They're more commonly found in higher-income markets or at larger, newer locations.

Considering a Home Sauna or Cold Plunge?

If you're specifically joining a gym for sauna access and your local Anytime Fitness doesn't have one, it may be worth considering a home alternative. Infrared sauna cabinets start around $1,000–$1,500 and pay for themselves within a few years compared to a gym membership. You can also build a DIY cold plunge for under $400 using a chest freezer or stock tank — an increasingly popular complement to home sauna use.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Anytime Fitness have a sauna?
Some locations do, but the majority don't. Anytime Fitness is a franchise, so amenities vary significantly by owner and location. Call your local club to confirm before joining.
Does Anytime Fitness have tanning beds?
Many Anytime Fitness locations have tanning beds, though it's usually an add-on cost. Tanning is more widely available at Anytime Fitness locations than saunas.
Can I use any Anytime Fitness location?
Yes. Most standard Anytime Fitness memberships include reciprocal use at other locations in the U.S. and even internationally. But amenities vary — just because your home club has a sauna doesn't mean a visited club will.
How much does Anytime Fitness cost?
Membership prices are set by each franchisee, but typically range from $30–$50/month. Joining fees and contract terms also vary by location.
