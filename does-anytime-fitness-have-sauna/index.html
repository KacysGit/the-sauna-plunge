Does Anytime Fitness Have a Sauna?

Quick Answer

Some Anytime Fitness locations have saunas, but it's not standard across all clubs. Because Anytime Fitness is a franchise, amenities vary significantly by owner. Call your specific location or check the club's page on the Anytime Fitness website to confirm before joining.

Does Anytime Fitness Have a Sauna?

Anytime Fitness is a 24-hour gym franchise with over 4,700 locations globally. Because each club is independently owned, amenity offerings vary substantially. Sauna availability is not a guaranteed feature — you'll need to verify with your specific location.

What Type of Sauna Does Anytime Fitness Have?

Anytime Fitness operates on a franchise model, meaning each club is independently owned. Some franchise owners invest in sauna facilities; others don't. There's no chain-wide standard for sauna availability. Urban locations and newer clubs tend to be more likely to have saunas.

Anytime Fitness Membership Cost and Sauna Access

Anytime Fitness memberships typically run $30–50/month depending on location. Access to amenities including any sauna is included with the standard membership where available.

Alternatives If Your Anytime Fitness Doesn't Have a Sauna

Not every location is created equal — amenities vary by individual club, age of facility, and local franchise decisions. If your nearest Anytime Fitness doesn't have a sauna, the best alternatives are:

  • LA Fitness: Most locations have a dry sauna and/or steam room. Widely considered the most reliable sauna gym chain in the US.
  • YMCA: Many branches have saunas — call your local Y to confirm. Day passes often available.
  • 24 Hour Fitness (Sport/Super Sport): Upper-tier memberships include sauna access at most facilities.
  • Life Time Fitness / Equinox: Premium chains that consistently offer sauna, steam, and sometimes cold plunge. Higher membership cost but excellent facilities.
  • Day spa or Korean spa: $20–50 day pass for unlimited sauna access without a membership.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does every Anytime Fitness location have a sauna?
No — sauna availability varies by location. Always call or check the specific club's amenity list on the Anytime Fitness website before joining specifically for sauna access.
How do I find a gym with a sauna near me?
Search "gym with sauna near me" on Google Maps, then filter for higher-tier chains (LA Fitness, YMCA, 24 Hour Fitness, Life Time). Check individual location pages for amenities, and always confirm by phone before visiting.
